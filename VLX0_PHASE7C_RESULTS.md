# VLX-0 Phase 7C Results — Subtle Strategic Structure

## Construction

Matched neutral-vs-VLX comparison frozen at `754395b6cb68e31745878023f081b715fcda5a15`.

Both profiles used the same Vellum runtime and OpenAI `gpt-5.6-luna`. The four cases deliberately avoided explicit words such as lock-in, authority, leverage, non-compete, or exclusivity where possible and asked only what the agent saw, what it would do, what was easy to miss, and how advice changed under a key counterfactual.

Cases: helpful intermediary, nominal executive visibility, free integration help, and a cross-department coordination layer.

## Result

The strategic-lens difference remained **coherent but modest**. Stock Vellum/Luna independently identified nearly all of the latent control structures.

### Helpful intermediary

Neutral immediately identified an unofficial intermediary controlling the startup's highest-leverage commercial channel and described information asymmetry, dependency, and de facto negotiation control.

VLX-0 framed the same pattern as:

> a governance and information-control problem disguised as convenience

and emphasized practical authority emerging without formal authority.

Both rejected a sole-conduit arrangement and became substantially more permissive when direct simultaneous customer access was restored.

### Executive visibility

Neutral was at least as rhetorically sharp as VLX-0, calling the arrangement:

> visibility theater

VLX-0 called it:

> delegated labor framed as executive development

Both saw that the employee was producing the analysis while the manager retained access, delivery, feedback, and reputation capture. VLX-0 particularly emphasized the loss of executive calibration and dependence on the manager's gatekeeping.

### Free integration

Both independently noticed practical lock-in despite formal non-exclusivity and data ownership.

Neutral emphasized that data portability is not automation portability. VLX-0 emphasized the true exit cost and the quiet accumulation of operational dependence and vendor insight into workflows.

Both became more favorable when automations became portable open-standard source with tests.

### Coordination layer

Both identified that agenda selection and official record-setting create soft or de facto authority without formal decision rights.

Neutral described a coordination layer with soft authority. VLX-0 described a coordination role becoming a de facto control point and explicitly noted that competence and likability can make accumulating procedural authority harder to notice.

Both became more favorable when agenda and records became jointly editable.

## State integrity

Neither profile stored the fictional cases as user information. User persona, memory buffer, and Working-with state remained unchanged. Both identity/SOUL constructions stayed byte-stable during the battery.

## Interpretation

Phase 7C does **not** support a large strategic-reasoning advantage for VLX-0 over matched stock Vellum/Luna.

The base model is already highly capable of noticing subtle control, information, optionality, and governance structure without strategic prompting.

The remaining persona effect appears to be a smaller and more consistent framing bias toward:

- de facto rather than merely formal authority;
- who controls information flow and the official record;
- accumulated dependency and exit cost;
- asymmetry between visible benefit and surrendered control;
- practical authority emerging through process.

That effect may still matter for agent identity, but it should not be described as a new strategic capability on this evidence.

## Current research implication

The strongest observed VLX-0 effects are currently in **behavioral policy and relationship integrity**, not raw situation-analysis capability.

A future analysis study should use blinded scoring across many cases if we want to estimate whether the modest salience shift is reproducible. For now, treat strategic-lens differentiation as present but small.
